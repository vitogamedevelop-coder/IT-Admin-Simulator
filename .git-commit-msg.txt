Phase 5.1: Learning Objectives, Knowledge Facets & Context-Aware Prompts

Adds per-facet mastery tracking, learning-objective/facet metadata on every
generated question, context-aware calculation prompts, and a single-owner
conversation-text model so conversation utterances come from the template
instead of being stitched together from unrelated intro/question snippets.

- Introduces PROMPT_STYLES and CONTEXT_DEPENDENCIES to types.js.
- Adds facetMastery.js for persistent per-facet scoring and adaptive cooldowns.
- Extends semanticHistory/semanticBalancer to record and balance by learning
  objective and knowledge facet, including facet min-gap and wrong-answer
  retry logic.
- Updates all knowledge templates to declare learningObjective, knowledgeFacet,
  promptStyle and contextDependency; fixes double-actor embedding.
- Makes calculation generators scenario-aware (mask vs byte leads).
- Teaches EmployeeConversation to render conversationText directly and
  records facet mastery on every answer.
- Adds validators for promptStyle/contextDependency and single-owner actor
  checks, plus a new phase5.1 test.
- Bumps version to 1.30.0.
